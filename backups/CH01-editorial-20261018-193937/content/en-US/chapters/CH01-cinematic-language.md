## Key terms {#CH01-S03}

### Deep focus（深焦攝影） {#TERM-CH01-DEEP-FOCUS}

`TERM-CH01-DEEP-FOCUS`  
**Definition:** Foreground and background elements at different distances remain legibly sharp; results depend on optics, aperture, lighting, capture medium, and viewing conditions.  
**繁體中文：** 前景、背景等不同距離的元素同時保持可辨識的清晰度；效果受光學、光圈、照明、感光材料與觀看條件影響。

### Multiple perspectives（多重觀點敘事） {#TERM-CH01-MULTIPLE-PERSPECTIVE}

`TERM-CH01-MULTIPLE-PERSPECTIVE`  
**Definition:** Different characters or information ranges present an event from distinct viewpoints; difference alone does not prove that a narrator is lying.  
**繁體中文：** 以不同人物或資訊範圍呈現同一事件；觀點不同本身不能證明敘述者說謊。

### Offscreen space（畫外空間） {#TERM-CH01-OFFSCREEN-SPACE}

`TERM-CH01-OFFSCREEN-SPACE`  
**Definition:** Space beyond the frame that viewers may infer through sound, eyelines, movement, or editing relations.  
**繁體中文：** 畫框之外、可由聲音、視線、動作或剪輯關係推知的空間。

### Long take（長鏡頭） {#TERM-CH01-LONG-TAKE}

`TERM-CH01-LONG-TAKE`  
**Definition:** A single uncut shot of comparatively extended duration; there is no universal duration threshold across films.  
**繁體中文：** 持續時間較長且內部未剪接的單一鏡頭；沒有跨作品通用的秒數門檻。

### Frame within a frame（框中框構圖） {#TERM-CH01-FRAME-WITHIN-FRAME}

`TERM-CH01-FRAME-WITHIN-FRAME`  
**Definition:** A doorway, window, or other in-frame boundary creates a secondary frame that changes visibility, occlusion, or spatial layering.  
**繁體中文：** 利用門、窗等畫面內邊界形成次級框景，改變可見範圍、遮擋或空間層次。

### Layered composition（層次構圖） {#TERM-CH01-LAYERED-COMPOSITION}

`TERM-CH01-LAYERED-COMPOSITION`  
**Definition:** Depth, foreground/background, reflections, or occlusion arrange multiple visible information layers; this is not a fixed shot type or guaranteed psychological effect.  
**繁體中文：** 以景深、前後景、反射或遮擋安排多組可見資訊；不等同固定鏡頭類型或必然心理效果。

### Case 1: *Citizen Kane* (1941) — Multiple Information Layers in One Frame {#CH01-P001}

The British Film Institute (BFI) identifies deep-focus photography, abstracted camera angles, non-chronological narration, and overlapping dialogue among the film’s formal features.[R013](#ref-R013) In a separate feature, the BFI discusses a deep-focus shot and notes cinematographer Gregg Toland’s shared credit with the director.[R020](#ref-R020) These are analytical choices, not a claim that every shot uses deep focus or that it can be reduced to one fixed camera setting.

**AI exercise (practical recommendation):** In an original classroom, place a teacher in the foreground, students in the middle ground, and a person by a distant doorway. Sketch each plane’s purpose before testing legibility, viewer attention, and continuity. Record actual parameters; do not present the exercise as a recreation of the film.

### Case 2: *Rashomon* (1950) — Perspective and Information {#CH01-P002}

The BFI describes events retold from different perspectives, with accounts that do not fully agree. This supports discussion of viewpoint, testimony, and how information reaches viewers.[R014](#ref-R014) Different perspectives do not automatically mean every account is equally reliable or establish a universal law of audience psychology.

**AI exercise (practical recommendation):** Invent a classroom equipment accident. Present it from a teacher’s, a student’s, and a distant observer’s position. First record the event facts, then list what each character can see. Compare how editing order changes interpretation without changing the underlying event.

### Case 5: *Yi Yi* (2000) — City Life, Reflection, and Layered Visibility {#CH01-P005}

A Criterion essay discusses Taipei city life, visual overlaps between foreground and background, windows, and reflected cityscapes as ways of relating characters to their surroundings.[R019](#ref-R019) This is a critic’s formal interpretation, not a claim that every reflection carries the same meaning.

**AI exercise (practical recommendation):** In an original school corridor, compose reflective glass with activity both inside and outside. Sketch camera, glass, people, and reflection geometry; inspect reflection direction, occlusion, and continuity. If a video model cannot keep the reflection stable, separate it into a controllable composite layer or use a diagram.
