# CH11 human review checklist

本清單是開啟中的審稿事項，不代表已完成簽核。

| Item | Status | Evidence needed | Responsible reviewer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Film-directing history and theory | NEEDS HUMAN REVIEW | Verify appropriate scholarship, edition, page locators, cross-cultural scope, and claims about auteurism/collaboration | Film historian/editor |
| Traditional directing practice | NEEDS HUMAN REVIEW | Confirm responsibilities fit the project’s production context and avoid treating one industry as universal | Practicing director / production editor |
| DGA agreement statements | NEEDS HUMAN REVIEW | Verify 2023–2026 handbook wording and applicable scope; obtain jurisdiction-specific legal advice if needed | Rights/legal editor |
| English translation and terms | NEEDS HUMAN REVIEW | Line edit meaning, tone, role names, performance language, and consistency against Traditional Chinese source | Bilingual film editor |
| The Godfather case | NEEDS HUMAN REVIEW | Select lawful edition; log edition/source/viewer/timecodes; verify cited descriptions and interpretation | Author / film researcher |
| AI workflow and confidentiality advice | NEEDS HUMAN REVIEW | Check current tool terms and privacy policy only for tools actually selected; no tool capability is claimed as verified | AI production editor / privacy reviewer |
| CH11-FIG-001 and CH11-FIG-002 | NEEDS HUMAN REVIEW | Confirm creator attribution, rights, similarity, typography, accessibility, print and web rendering | Author / rights / design editor |
| Author approval | NEEDS HUMAN REVIEW | Record approval against a specific revision and scope | Author |
| Publication readiness | BLOCKED | Resolve all above, run final proof, and record required approvals | Editorial lead |
