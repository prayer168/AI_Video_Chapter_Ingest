# CH09 來源與案例查證紀錄

查核日期：2026-10-21。網頁書目查核不等同完整閱讀紙本／電子書，也不等同本專案觀看片。未核對書籍頁碼或電影版次、片段 timecode。

- R066 Rick Altman, *Film/Genre*：出版社書目頁核對書名、出版資訊及類型研究範圍；語義／句法／語用框架引用以學術用法為工作摘要，需電影理論審校。https://www.bloomsbury.com/us/filmgenre-9780851707174/
- R067 Jason Mittell, *Complex TV: The Poetics of Contemporary Television Storytelling*：NYU Press 書目頁核對書名與研究範圍；系列敘事及觀眾理解論述需對照全文。https://nyupress.org/9780814759564/complex-tv/
- R068 James Mottram, “Non-linear Nolan,” BFI, 2020-08-28：二手評論，用於案例查證入口；《Memento》《Dunkirk》觀片版本與時間碼待作者補錄。https://www.bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound/features/non-linear-nolan
- R069 Artur Borowiecki, “Plotline terminology in film studies …,” 2024, DOI 10.31648/mcsc.9162：期刊文章摘要及 DOI 登錄可核；論文全文與術語採用須專業審讀。https://doi.org/10.31648/mcsc.9162
- R051 David Bordwell, “Three Dimensions of Film Narrative” (existing project ref): supporting narrative distinction; exact locator preserved as existing registry records.
- R062 Jason Mittell existing project ref: reused only as a cross-reference; bibliographic identity must be checked against R067 during deduplication.

案例用途均為分析入口，非唯一解讀或本專案觀片結論。不得補造 timecode、版次、逐字對白或研究頁碼。
